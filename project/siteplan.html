Wasatch Trail Trekkers

A website plan by Ken Dash

Site Name

Wasatch Trail Trekkers

I chose this name because the site is for a hiking club centered on Utah's Wasatch Front, and “Trekkers” signals an active, get-outside community rather than a passive brochure site. It's short, easy to say out loud at a trailhead, and makes the geographic focus obvious.

Optional domain availability: wasatchtrailtrekkers.org

Site Purpose

Wasatch Trail Trekkers gives Utah hikers one place to find trail recommendations by difficulty and season, practical gear guidance, and a schedule of upcoming group hikes — so both brand-new and experienced hikers can get out into the Wasatch mountains safely and with company.

Scenarios

Questions a site visitor might be asking that the content needs to answer:

  1. “I'm new to hiking and bringing my kids along — which nearby trail is short, safe, and family-friendly?”
  2. “When and where is the next group hike, and how do I sign up?”
  3. “What gear do I actually need to stay safe on a winter hike in the Wasatch mountains?”

Color Schema

A deep forest green paired with a warm trail-clay accent on a soft parchment background, evoking mountain trails and topographic maps while staying easy to read. This document uses the schema below.

  • Forest Green #2f5233 — headings, header/footer background, navigation
  • Trail Clay #9c4a1a — links, buttons, accents
  • Parchment #f6f3ea — page background
  • White #ffffff — card backgrounds
  • Charcoal #2b2a25 — body text

Typography

Two Google Fonts, kept deliberately simple:

Oswald — headings and navigation Aa Bb Cc 0123

Nunito Sans — body text and captions Aa Bb Cc 0123

Wireframes

Home page layout, mobile and desktop views. These are structural only — no final content or colors.
